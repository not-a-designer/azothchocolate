# Azoth Photography Brief

The current people-focused tasting photographs are temporary. Replace them with authentic Azoth
photography as soon as a staged tasting can be documented. The founder section uses a real interim
portrait of Joel; replace it with the planned environmental portrait when that shoot is available.
Chocolate shown in every image should be plain bars, scored bar segments, or naturally broken
pieces—never dipped truffles, bonbons, filled chocolates, or decorative confectionery.

## Visual direction

- Warm natural side light, real venue texture, and candid expressions.
- Documentary editorial framing rather than posed event photography.
- A mix of wide atmosphere, medium interactions, and close sensory details.
- Clothing and surfaces in chocolate brown, cream, terracotta, saffron, berry, and botanical olive.
- No visible third-party trademarks, artificial classroom staging, or oversized desserts.
- Obtain releases from everyone whose face will be published.
