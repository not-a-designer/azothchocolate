About Azoth Chocolate

Chocolate is worth
paying attention to.

Azoth creates approachable, educational tasting experiences centered on fine chocolate and the people, places, and processes behind it.

Each pairing is designed in collaboration with the host venue, connecting chocolate with coffee, beer, or wine through flavor, fermentation, origin, and craft.

Led by Joel Johnson

Wisconsin native formerly based in Albuquerque, New Mexico, Joel brings a maker's perspective to approachable tasting experiences that connect craft, origin, and sensory education
