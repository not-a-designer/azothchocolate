About Azoth Chocolate

Chocolate is worth
paying attention to.

Azoth creates approachable, social tasting experiences centered on fine chocolate and the people, places, and processes behind it.

Each experience is designed with the host venue, exploring chocolate independently or in conversation with coffee, beer, or wine through flavor, fermentation, origin, and craft.

Led by Joel Johnson

Joel brings a maker’s perspective to every tasting - grounding the experience in craft, origin, and sensory education while keeping it warm, conversational, and approachable.
